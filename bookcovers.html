Evolution of the Book Cover for San Felipe Creek

We're going to talk about AI here, so tighten up your wig.

AI is a terrible and wonderful thing at the same time. While most of the writing community melts down over the use of AI to generate books, and rightfully so, the use of AI as an editor, and as an image generator is unparalleled.

For me, creating the first book cover was something I couldn't afford to pay someone to do. It was in the thousands of dollars. Paying for any one of three levels of editing was going to cost in the neighborhood $3,000.00. Believe me, I'd get the airconditioning fixed on my truck, first. I'm not wealthy. I was already using ChatGPT to send my paragraphs and chapters into so they could be checked for spelling, smoothing, and to catch as much of my poor punctuation as it could. It became early entertainment for me to take my chapters, feed it into the AI with the query "Please illustrate this for me. My characters in are junior high, and have this kind of hair, blah blah blah." It made some great things for me to print and stick in picture frames to adorn my writing table.


So, not only did poor Michael look like he wasn't only going to stumble, but he was going to fall on a 25-28 year-old Trish. Can we say, "Awkward much?" Keep in mind, the text is something I overlaid in Corel PhotoPaint. AI imagined my characters from the portion of the book I had already written. And they are sitting on the wrong side of the creek. In Del Rio at Lions Park, you couldn't sit on that side in the 1970s; it was a wild jungle of thorny bushes there.
