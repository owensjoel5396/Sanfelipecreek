Evolution of the Book Cover for San Felipe Creek

AI is a terrible and wonderful thing at the same time. While most of the writing community melts down over the use of AI to generate books, and rightfully so, the use of AI as an editor, and as an image generator is unparalleled.

For me, creating the first book cover was something I couldn't afford to pay someone to do. I was already using it to send my paragraphs and chapters into so they could be checked for spelling, smoothing, and to catch as much of my poor punctuation as it could. It became early entertainment for me to take my first chapter, feed it into the AI with the query "Please illustrate this for me. My characters in are junior high, and have this kind of hair, blah blah blah."


So, not only did poor Michael look like he wasn't only going to stumble, but he was going to fall on a 25-28 year-old Trish.
